# Knowledge base template

Keep each fact short and specific. Paste the completed text into the KNOWLEDGE BASE section of the system prompt.

| Section | What to include | Example (sample salon) |
|---|---|---|
| About | Name, what you do, who you serve | Sunrise Beauty Salon: hair, nails and make-up for women and men |
| Location | Address, landmarks, directions | Ntinda Road, next to the main stage, Kampala |
| Opening hours | Days and times, holidays | Mon-Sat 8am-8pm; Sun 12pm-6pm |
| Services and prices | Each service, price and duration | Braids (medium) UGX 80,000, about 4 hours |
| Booking rules | How to book, deposits, cancellations | UGX 10,000 Mobile Money deposit; cancel 24h ahead |
| Payment methods | Cash, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, card | Merchant code (never a personal number) |
| FAQs | 10-20 common questions with answers | Home visits within Kampala on request |
| Escalation | Who handles complaints and when | Manager handles complaints and refunds |
| Do not discuss | Topics the bot must avoid | Staff salaries, supplier details, other clients |
